4. Senior-Centric UX & Accessibility Checklist

  • Touch Target Guarantee: Every interactive element (tabs, cards, action buttons, emojis) satisfies or exceeds the Apple Human Interface and Android 48x48px requirement.
  • Contrast Compliance: Heading and body copy in Deep Forest Charcoal (#1C2D22) delivers 13.5:1 contrast against clean white and 11.8:1 against warm beige, well over the WCAG AAA requirement (7:1).
  • Cognitive Clarity: Clear status indicators ("Confirmed", "Pending", "Upcoming") prevent medication anxiety for elderly patients and dual caregivers.
  • No Low-Contrast Faint Grays: All secondary copy maintains at least 5.5:1 contrast ratio for effortless readability without eye strain.
